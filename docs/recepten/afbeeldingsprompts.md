# Receptillustraties

Gegenereerd met de ingebouwde image_gen-tool. Originele PNG-bestanden blijven bewaard in de Codex-map; de app gebruikt WebP met behoud van transparantie.

## Gedeelde prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: CocoKiki children's cookbook finished dish thumbnail. Warm softly painted semi-realistic storybook food illustration, tactile natural edible textures, muted natural colors, warm ivory ceramic bowl. Three-quarter elevated view, entire bowl centered and completely visible with generous transparent padding, matching scale across the recipe collection. Soft diffuse lighting, subtle contact shadow only. Isolated on genuinely transparent background. No text, no lettering, no people, no hands, no utensils, no raw ingredients around the bowl, no scenery. Show only ingredients specified in the dish. Square composition.
