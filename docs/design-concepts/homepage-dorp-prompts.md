# Knus dorp op de homepage

Gegenereerd met de ingebouwde image_gen-tool, met het vertrouwde 3D-wereldconcept als stijlreferentie. De app gebruikt twee WebP-bestanden met transparante lucht: public/dreamworld/village/landscape.webp en portrait.webp.

## Basisprompt

Production background illustration for CocoKiki cozy village homepage, warm soft dimensional 3D handcrafted children's game, established wooden treehouse, authentic artisan bakery, arched timber glass greenhouse, cozy school workshop, explorer cottage with globe and brass telescope. Muted moss, sage, warm cream plaster, oak, terracotta, blue-green water, warm amber windows. Exactly FIVE main destinations all standing on ONE CONTINUOUS gently rolling grassy landscape with connecting sandy footpaths around a small central clearing. NOT floating islands, no planets, no separated pedestals, no cliff underside. A winding narrow brook along right edge with one small timber footbridge, a bench, modest shrubs, few flowers, quiet welcoming lived-in village. Broad canopy trees at outer edges, do not obscure buildings or paths. No people, no animals, no signs, no UI, no labels, no logo, no text. Elevated front three-quarter camera, all entrances facing the viewer, buildings distinct and separated, no overlapping silhouettes. Full composition fits within frame with margins. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT SKY ABOVE AND AROUND THE VILLAGE SILHOUETTE, not a sky painted in. Landscape fills bottom frame naturally, no floating rock base. Retain soft realistic wood grain, gently rounded shapes, warm lamps, not flat vector, not sci-fi.
